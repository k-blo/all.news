About all.news

The world's headlines, one page.

all.news is a neutral news aggregator. It collects headlines from more than a thousand news outlets in over 40 countries and dozens of languages, and links every headline straight to the original article on the publisher's site. We do not host, rewrite or summarise articles.

Sections

  • News — general news, filterable by country, language and outlet. Every country and language also has its own page under /news/.
  • Space, Business, Tech and Gaming — topic feeds from specialist outlets worldwide, filterable by language and outlet.

How it works

Our crawler reads the public RSS feeds and news sitemaps that publishers provide for exactly this purpose. Only articles published today are shown; each link appears once, even if several feeds carry it. Past days stay available in the archive.

Which outlets we include

We aim to be neutral rather than curate the "right" news: if a reputable news index lists an outlet, it is a candidate for us too. We only include outlets whose feeds are technically usable and whose robots.txt allows us to read them, and we exclude sources where the law of the countries we serve requires it.

Using the site

  • Filter by country, language and outlet. Your choice is saved in your own browser (local storage), not on our servers.
  • Hide an outlet with the eye icon next to the time; undo it under Filter → Medias.
  • Search filters today's headlines by keyword or outlet name.
  • Share copies a link that highlights the headline on all.news.
  • Settings switch between dark and light mode and a compact layout.

For publishers

Our crawler identifies itself as AllNewsBot and follows robots.txt. It fetches only feeds and sitemaps, never full articles, and uses conditional requests (ETag / Last-Modified) to keep load minimal. To have your outlet added, corrected or removed, contact us via the details in the imprint; we act on removal requests promptly.
