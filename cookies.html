Cookie Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Local storage works similarly but is never sent to a server. This page lists what is used on www.all.news. For details on how the data is processed, see the privacy policy.

Storage set by all.news

all.news itself sets no cookies. It uses your browser's storage only to remember your choices. This is strictly necessary for the features you use and needs no consent.

NameTypePurposeKept
allnews.country, allnews.lang, allnews.excludeLocal storageYour country, language and outlet filters on the news feedUntil you clear it
allnews.<section>.lang, allnews.<section>.excludeLocal storageLanguage and outlet filters of the Space, Business, Tech and Gaming sectionsUntil you clear it
allnews.welcomedLocal storageRemembers that you saw the welcome messageUntil you clear it
allnews.theme, allnews.densityLocal storageDark/light mode and compact layout from SettingsUntil you clear it
allnews.query, allnews.<section>.querySession storageYour search text for the current visitUntil you close the tab

Cookies set by third parties

These are only set where you consented, if consent is required where you live (EEA, UK, Switzerland).

Managing your choices

  • Consent: visitors from the EEA, UK and Switzerland see Google's consent message on their first visit. Reopen it any time with Cookie settings in the footer.
  • Personalised ads: turn them off in Google Ads Settings or via aboutads.info and youronlinechoices.eu.
  • Analytics: install Google's Analytics opt-out add-on.
  • Your browser: block or delete cookies and site data in its settings. Clearing site data also resets your all.news filters and settings.
